FAQ

Questions owners ask me

How long does a website take?

A ready-made site with your details usually takes 2-3 days. A custom website takes 5-10 days depending on pages and features.

Do I get my own domain?

Yes. I help you buy a domain in your name (roughly ₹500-1000 a year, paid to the registrar) and connect it. The site and domain belong to you.

How does payment work?

50% advance to start and 50% after you approve the final site. I share payment details directly on WhatsApp.

Can I change things later?

Custom plans include 7 days of free edits. After that, small changes like text, prices and photos are quick and affordable.

How will customers contact me through the site?

The enquiry or booking form opens WhatsApp with the customer's details already filled in, so every lead lands on your phone.

I already have a Google or Facebook page. Do I still need a website?

Yes. A website is yours, ranks on Google, shows your prices and photos properly, and helps you get direct bookings instead of paying marketplace commission.
